1 Start here

Melee Zone organizes X post submissions, assigned reviews, MC rewards, community task threads, quizzes and raffle access. Moderators also give private, evidence-based support to reviewers and, when assigned, content members.

Open the existing Melee Zone panel in Discord for member actions. Select Moderator support for your weekly support tasks, or use /support panel. If you have bot-admin access, /admin_panel opens the Admin Control Center.

Access matters. The Meleeionaires reviewer role allows assigned reviewing. The volunteer moderator role allows your own support workload. Neither automatically grants MC-award tools or access to everyone's reports. Use only the actions your assigned role permits; ask the lead admin if access is missing.

Read the confirmation after every action. A private response is visible to you in Discord, but actions can still change member balances, roles or task status. Keep personal evaluations and member reports within the authorized team.

3 Submissions and results

Help a member submit a post

  1. Ask them to register their X handle in Dashboard → Register X Handle if required.
  2. Open Submission → Submit Post.
  3. Paste a direct X or Twitter post URL into X / Twitter Post URL.
  4. Read the confirmation, then use My Last Submissions to check the saved post.

A profile link is not a post link. The same member cannot submit the same post again in another week; different tracking parameters do not make it a new post. Reviewers check that the post belongs to the submitter. A valid link or registered handle is not proof of ownership.

Submission slots reset with the Saturday cycle. Use the remaining-slots count in the panel instead of promising a fixed number. Filling the full weekly submission allowance can earn the displayed completion reward once for that week.

Read submission results

My Last Submissions shows saved post links, status, available scores and recorded post MC. Previous and Next browse the recent history.

StatusMeaning
PendingThe submission is waiting for review work to progress.
AssignedReview assignments have been created; the result is not final yet.
ApprovedThe required review result meets the approval threshold.
RejectedThe final score does not meet approval. A lower reward band can still apply.

Post MC depends on the final average and the server's reward rules. Showcase requires its own threshold and can add Golden MC. A private moderator support score is a different measure and does not determine the post's reward.

4 Assigned reviews

Complete an assigned review

  1. Open Reviews → Assignments. Read the post link, assignment ID and remaining time.
  2. Open the post and check its content, context and author.
  3. Choose Submit a Review.
  4. Enter the assignment ID shown in your list, an integer score from 1 to 10, and specific written feedback. Add an X comment URL only if you actually left that comment.
  5. Submit once and read the saved confirmation and progress count.
Form fieldWhat to enter
Assignment ID (first 8 chars)The short ID from your own active assignment list.
Score (1-10)An integer supported by what you read.
Your Written FeedbackExplain what works and what could improve. Meet the form's length requirement.
X comment URL (optional bonus)A direct link to your relevant X comment. Leave blank if there is none.

Only the assigned reviewer can complete that active assignment. Already completed or reassigned work cannot be submitted again. The Reviews panel shows the current review goal and its MC reward. Comment-bonus eligibility is separate and requires the relevant comment links on the week's reviews.

Deadlines and work you cannot review

Use the assignment's displayed deadline. A warning indicates unfinished work; after the applicable grace period, the bot may offer replacement work to an eligible reviewer. If your assignment has been reassigned, stop using the old ID and refresh your list.

Ready for More joins the extra-review queue. It does not create a post on demand or guarantee immediate work. Finish your primary reviews first. Extra rescue work has its own deadline and capacity limit.

If a post is inaccessible, creates a conflict of interest, or cannot be reviewed fairly because of language, use /review_skip with its assignment_id and a reason. The bot returns that assignment and tries to find a replacement. Skipping is not a completed review and does not earn its completion credit.

For authorized admins: /admin_panel → Content → Distribute Reviews fills available review assignments. It does not create volunteer moderator support tasks or replenish member submission slots. Use Overview or /stats to check current work before intervening.

5 Weekly moderator support

Choose a workload you can manage

Open Moderator support in the main panel or use /support panel. Support tasks are people whose recorded work from the previous week you will review. These are separate from the X posts assigned to you as a reviewer.

ControlWhat it does
I am availableOpts you into reviewer support at the standard capacity, normally up to ten people per week. It does not opt you into a separate member workload.
Choose a person to supportOpens one pending person's saved evidence and shows whether the task concerns a reviewer or a content member.
Next tasksCycles through pending task pages, with up to ten people per page.
Pause my workloadStops your availability without penalty and passes unfinished tasks to volunteers with room. Completed work stays recorded.
Team messagesOpens your private inbox, including replies to your feedback.

To choose your own capacity, use /support availability. For example, reviewers:5 members:0 available:true volunteers for up to five reviewer tasks and no member tasks. Reviewer and member capacities are separate: ten reviewer slots plus five member slots can mean fifteen people.

Weekly allocation balances available capacity and varies who supports each person. No one evaluates themselves. With fifty reviewers and five available moderators offering ten slots each, there is capacity for ten people per moderator. If there is less capacity, some work waits; the bot does not force extra tasks onto a full moderator.

Finish or skip a support task

  1. Select the person and confirm the task's scope.
  2. Read the saved samples and follow their links when accessible. The samples describe recorded work, not the person's entire contribution.
  3. Choose Score and feedback.
  4. Enter an integer score from 1 to 10, the full IDs of the samples you actually checked, and one evidenced strength plus one helpful suggestion.
  5. Submit once. The feedback is saved in the recipient's Team messages; an optional DM may notify them.

The form fields are Score 1-10. Blank only if no samples exist, Full IDs of samples you checked, and One strength and one helpful suggestion. Sample IDs are pre-filled; remove any you did not check. Feedback accepts 30 to 1,500 characters.

Use Skip without penalty for a conflict, unavailable evidence or lack of time. The task moves to another eligible volunteer only if they have capacity; otherwise it waits. The skip makes no MC deduction and records no negative score. A task that is no longer assigned to you must not be scored from an old panel.

No samples: leave the score blank and write a friendly check-in, or skip. Missing evidence is not zero. A rating based on fewer samples is marked as limited evidence. Support scores do not automatically change MC or roles.

6 Fair scores and useful feedback

Score the sampled work, not the person's personality, friendship with the team, popularity or message volume. A post score assesses content. A moderator support score assesses the quality of the sampled reviews or member work. Keep those measures separate.

ScoreReviewer supportMember content support
1 to 2Substantially inaccurate, unrelated or harmful feedback, with concrete evidence.The sampled content substantially misses its purpose.
3 to 4Mostly generic feedback with little usable explanation.An idea is present, but relevance, clarity or support needs work.
5 to 6A usable review with some explanation and a clear next improvement.Relevant, useful content with identifiable improvements.
7 to 8Specific, balanced and actionable feedback supported by the samples.Clear, useful content with adequate supporting detail.
9 to 10Consistently excellent sampled reviews with precise, helpful examples.Excellent sampled work with clear value and strong supporting examples.

Different scores between reviewers are a reason to examine the explanation, not proof of misconduct. Repeated phrases are also a prompt to check context. Do not demand more output simply to justify a higher score.

Write feedback the person can use

Name one thing that worked and one small, achievable improvement. Tie both to a checked sample. Avoid labels such as lazy, bad reviewer or not committed.

Example: “Your review of sample [ID] clearly explains why the example supports the post. In the next review, add one specific suggestion the author could act on. That would make your feedback easier to use.”

When evidence is missing: “I could not find recorded samples for this period, so I have left the score blank. If you would like help finding your assignments or using the panel, reply here.”

Keep feedback private. Do not post scores in public channels, compare people publicly or use support scores as an informal punishment. If you cannot judge the work fairly, skip and tell the lead admin when context is needed.

7 Team messages and second looks

Read and reply to feedback

Open Team messages from the main panel, Reviews or Moderator support. /team_messages is a shortcut. This is a private inbox for saved team feedback and replies, not a public channel or a general direct-message composer.

ControlWhat it does
Choose a message to reply or request a second lookSelects a message and displays its full text. Select the intended message before replying.
ReplyOpens Your message and sends a saved private reply to that message's sender.
Request a second lookRequests an independent review of the selected evaluation. Explain what evidence or context should be reconsidered.
Older messagesMoves to an older page of messages.
Newest messagesReturns to the newest inbox page.
Keep feedback in the panel onlyTurns off DM notifications. Saved messages remain available in the panel.

Closed DMs do not remove saved feedback. To switch DM notifications back on, use /team_messages dm_notifications:true. Replies are limited to five per person per hour; if the limit is reached, wait before replying again.

Ordinary users cannot browse someone else's inbox. Authorized admins can review evaluations and reports. Do not describe this as absolute secrecy or include sensitive personal information.

Handle a request for a second look

The recipient selects the evaluation message, chooses Request a second look and provides a short reason. One request is allowed per evaluation. A second look applies to moderator support feedback; it is not a button for changing a post's final review result.

An authorized admin other than the evaluating moderator checks the evidence and records the explanation. The original rating stays in the record; any corrected score is recorded separately. The explanation appears in the member's private inbox. Neither a request nor its resolution changes MC automatically.

As the evaluating moderator, provide the checked samples and relevant context. Do not resolve your own evaluation's second-look request. Keep the exchange factual and give the member room to explain.

8 Giving and checking MC

Award MC to one or several members

These actions require bot-admin access. Open /admin_panel → Rewards → Award MC. Enter member mentions or Discord IDs, the amount, regular, golden or assignment, and a clear reason.

The amount is per recipient. Entering two members and 2 MC gives each member 2 MC, not 1 MC each. Up to 25 unique recipients are accepted. Repeated IDs count once. If a recipient is invalid, missing or a bot, the whole batch is rejected; correct the list before trying again.

/give_mc is the command equivalent. Select amount, reason, users or user, and mc_type. Multiple authorized admins can award at the same time. Agree who is awarding which contribution to avoid unintended extra credit.

A newly submitted award command or amount form is a new award, even if its text matches an earlier one. Before retrying after an uncertain result, check the recipient's balance or /mc_audit user:@member. A missing public notice does not by itself mean the reward failed.

Use a reaction or message shortcut

Use the team's approved reward emoji on a member's message in a readable channel or task thread. The reacting person must have bot-admin authority. Ordinary member reactions do not award MC. Bot messages and your own messages do not receive reaction MC from you.

Each admin can award once per message per approved emoji. Removing and adding the same reaction again does not pay again. Removing a reaction does not take the recorded MC back. Another authorized admin's reaction is a separate award; a different reward emoji can also be a separate award.

Message menu actionWhat it does
Apps → Award 1 MCGives 1 Regular MC to the message author. The same admin repeating this shortcut on the same message does not award it again.
Apps → Award MCOpens an amount-and-reason form for Regular MC to the message author. Each newly submitted form can give another award.

Open the message's context menu to find Apps. If an expected award is missing, collect the exact message link and ask an authorized admin to check the record before manually compensating.

Reward participants with Snapshot

  1. Go to the exact channel or thread you intend to reward and run /snapshot. The Snapshot button under Rewards points you to this command.
  2. Choose Regular, Golden or Assignment.
  3. Enter the look-back hours, MC amount per user and reason.
  4. Check the destination and preview. Choose Confirm & Award only when the recipients and amount are correct; otherwise choose Cancel.

Each distinct non-bot poster found in that scan receives the amount once. Snapshot is limited to 720 hours and up to 10,000 messages. Running it in a parent channel does not automatically include all child threads. A separate new snapshot can reward the same people again.

Check balances: members use Dashboard → My Balance or /my_mc. Authorized admins use /mc_audit for recent reward entries or /user_info for member details. Regular, Golden and Assignment balances add up to total MC.

9 Community tasks

Community tasks are thread-based activities. They are separate from assigned X reviews and weekly moderator support. Members post entries inside the task's thread, not in the parent task channel.

Authorized admins open /admin_panel → Content → Community Tasks.

ButtonWhat it does
New TaskOpens a task form with title, instructions, post cooldown and recurring yes/no. Saving creates the task thread.
Active TasksLists active task IDs, thread references, distinct participant counts and cooldowns.
Past TasksShows closed task records and participation counts.
Close TaskCloses the selected task ID. The form also asks whether to archive and lock its thread.

Check the exact task ID before closing it. Closing does not erase earlier MC. Participant count means distinct members, not the total number of messages or a quality score.

The thread's stated cooldown controls how often a member can post. Early posts can be removed with a cooldown notice. The bot's task cooldown exempts authorized bot admins; a moderator without that authority is still treated as a member. A separate Discord restriction can still block posting.

For an approved correction, an authorized admin can run /task_reset_cooldown user:@member in that task thread. This clears only that member's task cooldown, not their MC or participation record. A recurring label does not automatically create new tasks, pay rewards or close the thread.

10 Quiz and raffle

Help a member take the quiz

Open Quiz → Start Quiz. Read each question and choose the appropriate answer button. The result shows the score and recorded MC. My Quiz History shows completed results. A completed quiz cannot be repeated for another reward.

MC depends on the displayed score bands. A passing score of 70 percent can grant the quiz role. If an old question stops responding, reopen the Quiz panel and report the stalled attempt rather than promising a fresh rewarded attempt.

Authorized admins open /admin_panel → Content → Quiz for Active Quiz Info, User Progress and Export Quiz CSV. Load Quiz (JSON) publishes prepared quiz material; use it only when the lead has approved that material. Revoke All Quiz Roles removes the quiz role from its holders; it does not undo quiz results or MC. Obtain lead approval before a bulk removal.

Manage raffle access

Authorized admins open /admin_panel → Rewards → Raffle.

ButtonWhat it does
Give to UserAccepts space-separated member mentions or IDs and grants the raffle-access role.
Current HoldersShows who holds the raffle-access role.
Revoke AllRemoves the raffle-access role from all its holders. Use only for an approved bulk removal.

The team's designated raffle reaction can also grant raffle access to the message author when used by an authorized admin. Raffle access is a role, not MC or a guarantee of winning.

11 Reports and calculator

Participation and review support reports

These tools require bot-admin access. Open /admin_panel → Review support and analytics. Individual volunteer moderators cannot download everyone's records.

Operating buttonWhat it does
14-day reportRequests a private participation report for the preceding 14 days. Wait for completion before downloading.
Latest Excel and CSVDownloads the latest completed report, or shows its current progress.
CalculatorShows approximate workload and reward estimates from a completed report. It does not apply changes.
Refresh weekly allocationRefreshes eligible people and available volunteer work for the current support cycle. It is not a reroll for a preferred score.
Second-look requestsLists open evaluation-review requests for an independent authorized admin.
Pause supportPauses support scheduling and task scoring for everyone. Saved inboxes, reports, MC and the main review cycle remain. Use only with lead approval.

/review_analytics days:14 requests a report; /review_analytics_status checks or downloads one, using its returned job ID if needed. Automatic fortnightly reports remain private until an authorized admin requests the files.

The Excel workbook separates Overview, Calculator, Members, Reviewers, Moderators, Posts, Reviews and Oversight. CSV contains person-level participation data; detailed post and review records are in Excel. Import Discord IDs as Text when opening CSV in Excel.

Amber Calculator cells are editable estimates. Changing the workbook does not change the bot. Treat suggestions as approximations for the lead to consider, not mandatory quotas or promised MC. Actual awards and estimated rewards are separate.

For second-look resolution, a different authorized admin uses /support resolve with the request ID, an evidence-based explanation and, if appropriate, a revised score. The original evaluating moderator cannot resolve their own request.

Activity for one selected role

Open /admin_panel → Role report and select the intended role. The standard report examines a bounded 30-day window. It includes Discord IDs, usernames and display names, registered X handles when known, join dates, observed activity, recent message excerpts and MC balances.

Use /role_report_status with the report ID to check progress or download its workbook. An authorized admin can use /role_report for a different window, or /role_report_resume if a scan is paused and more history is needed.

Read History coverage before drawing conclusions. Counts cover accessible, recorded messages, not guaranteed lifetime activity. A blank handle means unknown; missing activity does not prove the person was inactive. Reading, voice participation, deleted content and inaccessible history may not be represented. Do not use message counts alone to judge review quality.

12 Member questions and suggested replies

Where do I start

“Open the Melee Zone panel and choose Guide and next steps. In Dashboard, register your X handle. My next steps will show what you can do next and any pending reviews.”

Why can I not submit a post

“Check Dashboard for your registered X handle, then open Submission to see your remaining weekly slots. Use a direct post link. Send me the exact error if the handle and slots look correct.”

Why is my link marked as a duplicate

“The same post can only be submitted once by you, even in another week. Changing the link's tracking text does not make it a new post. Check My Last Submissions for the earlier entry.”

Why can I not open Reviews

“Reviews is for the assigned reviewer role, usually Meleeionaires, and may require a registered X handle. Reopen the panel after checking those. I will ask the lead to check your access if it still refuses.”

Why do I have no assignments

“Open a fresh Reviews panel and check Assignments. Work depends on available posts and reviewer capacity. Ready for More joins the extra-review queue after your primary work is finished; it does not guarantee an immediate assignment.”

The assigned post is deleted or I cannot review it fairly

“Use /review_skip with that assignment ID and the closest reason. You do not need to invent a score for content you cannot assess. Send the assignment ID if the skip itself fails.”

Did my review save

“Check the saved confirmation and reopen Reviews to see your completed count. If the old assignment says completed or no longer active, do not send a duplicate. Give me the assignment ID and approximate submission time to check.”

Why did one completed review not give me MC

“The Reviews panel shows the weekly review goal and its reward. A single review does not necessarily earn that goal reward. Comment and post rewards follow separate rules. Let's check your progress and balance first.”

An admin reacted but my MC did not change

“Open Dashboard → My Balance and allow a short processing delay. Send the message link, emoji and approximate time if it is still missing. We will check the reward record before adding anything manually.”

Does removing a reaction remove MC

“No. A recorded reaction award stays. Re-adding the same emoji as the same admin does not pay again. Another authorized admin's reaction can be a separate award.”

Why was my community task message removed

“Post inside the task's thread, not its parent channel, and follow the cooldown shown in the task. If an accepted entry needs a correction, send the thread and message details so an authorized admin can check it.”

Does my moderator support score reduce my MC

“No. This is private feedback on the sampled work. It does not automatically change MC or roles. It is separate from the score reviewers give a submitted post.”

I disagree with the feedback or score

“Open Team messages, select that evaluation and choose Request a second look. Explain which sample or context should be reconsidered. A different authorized admin will review the request.”

I did not receive a feedback DM

“Open Team messages in the server panel. Feedback is saved there even if DMs are closed or notifications are off. You can reply from the inbox.”

Who can see my team feedback

“Your inbox is not visible to other ordinary members. Authorized admins can review evaluations and reports. Please keep sensitive personal information out of feedback messages.”

I passed the quiz but did not receive the role

“Check Quiz or My Quiz History for the saved result and MC. Send the quiz title and result to a moderator. A missing role needs an access check; you do not need to repeat a completed quiz.”

Why is my support score blank

“There were no recorded samples for that check-in, so the score was left blank. Blank is not zero and does not prove inactivity. You can reply with context or ask for help finding your saved work.”

I am a moderator and cannot finish this week's support work

“Use Pause my workload, or Skip without penalty for an individual task. Unfinished work moves only to volunteers with room. There is no MC penalty for taking a pause.”

Does the calculator change rewards automatically

“No. It shows estimates for the authorized team to consider. Editing its Excel inputs does not apply anything to the bot or create MC awards.”

13 Handling a problem

Check the result before repeating the action

  1. Reopen the relevant view from the main panel or its slash command. Old private buttons can expire.
  2. Check the member's role, X-handle requirement and current assignment or task status.
  3. For MC, check My Balance or ask an authorized admin to use /mc_audit. Do not assume a missing notice means a missing reward.
  4. If the issue continues, send the lead admin a short, private incident report. Do not promise a reward, role, deadline change or immediate resolution you cannot confirm.

For a missed reaction, an authorized admin can use /mc_reconcile in the affected channel or thread to recover eligible uncredited reactions. It checks a limited recent window, so it is not a complete historical audit. Already recorded reaction awards are not paid again by that check.

If a quiz role, task thread or report is unavailable, ask the lead to check access. Do not delete a quiz result, recreate an activity or send duplicate awards as a workaround.

Send a useful incident report

Include the member's Discord ID, exact channel or thread and message link, assignment or report ID when relevant, approximate time with timezone, button or command used, exact error, and the result you expected. Add a screenshot if it helps, without exposing another member's private feedback.

Suggested reply: “I have recorded the details and passed them to the lead admin. Please avoid repeating the award or submission while we check whether it already saved.”
