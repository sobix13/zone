# Melee Zone member and Meleeionaires guide

## Private team feedback

Open **Team messages** in the main panel or Reviews panel, or use `/team_messages`. Friendly suggestions stay in your private inbox even if DMs are closed. You can reply, turn DM notifications off or request a second look. A moderator's private 1–10 score describes checked samples; it is not your post's content score and does not automatically change your MC or roles. Missing samples are not scored as zero. You are not required to reply immediately. The [review support guide](REVIEW_SUPPORT.md) explains the evidence and appeal process.

## Your first five minutes

1. Open the server's Melee Zone panel or run `/onboard`.
2. Register your X handle in Dashboard or `/register_twitter`. Enter the handle without `@`.
3. Submit an original X post through Submission or `/submit` using its direct status URL.
4. If you hold the configured Meleeionaires reviewer role, open Reviews or `/my_reviews`.
5. Open Dashboard or `/my_mc` to see your balances. The guide's My next steps button shows the work you still have.

`/guide` opens a fresh introduction. `/onboard` resumes your saved page. You can use both even if the role-onboarding DM was blocked.

## Submitting useful work

Use one direct post URL per submission. Standard `x.com/user/status/ID`, `twitter.com/user/status/ID` and `x.com/i/status/ID` links are supported. Different query strings do not make the same post a new submission. A post you already submitted cannot be submitted again in another week.

Only submit work you can identify and explain. An `/i/status/` link does not reveal the author in its path, so a reviewer checks ownership. Your registered handle is not independently verified through X.

Your weekly capacity is shown in the panel. Completing the configured submission capacity can earn a once-per-week completion reward. A manual redistribution does not refill everyone's submission slots.

## Completing a review

Copy the assignment ID from Reviews. Open the assigned post and read it before deciding a score. Submit a score from 1 to 10 and feedback that explains the strengths, weaknesses and one useful improvement. The configured minimum feedback length applies.

You can use the panel form or `/review`. An X comment URL is optional unless a moderator sets another policy; it may count toward the existing comment bonus. The bot checks the URL shape, while moderators check whether the comment is meaningful.

Only your active assignment can be completed. A second click cannot create another review. Work reassigned to someone else is no longer yours. For an inaccessible post, conflict of interest or language issue, use `/review_skip`, choose a reason and return the assignment. Skipping does not earn a review reward.

The deadline shown beside your assignment is authoritative. Warnings and rescues run periodically. Finish your normal work before selecting Ready for More. Rescue assignments appear only when overdue work and eligible capacity exist.

## Understanding MC

Regular, golden and assignment balances are separate; together they form your total. Your server's settings determine exact amounts. Default post-score rewards are 0 below 4, 1 from 4 to below 6, 3 from 6 to below 8 and 5 from 8 through 10. Approval begins at 6. A rejected lower-tier post can still earn regular MC.

Showcase, completing the review goal, submission completion, comment bonuses, quizzes and explicit admin awards follow their own rules. Check the panel's current values instead of assuming every action gives one MC.

Admin reactions award the message author. Each authorized admin can award once per message and configured emoji. Two admins can give separate awards. Removing/re-adding the same reaction does not create another payment, and removing it does not erase previously recorded MC.

## Community tasks and quiz

Read the task instructions and post inside the task thread. The parent task channel is reserved for task organization. A member cooldown applies to accepted task entries; a second entry before the interval expires can be deleted. Wait before trying again. Admins can moderate without consuming that cooldown.

Open Quiz in the panel to take the active quiz. A quiz is completed once per member. Passing can give the configured pass role when the bot has permission to manage it. MC can be recorded even if role delivery fails; report that role problem without retaking for another reward.

Raffle access is a separate role. It does not by itself change your MC balance.

## Getting help

If MC looks wrong, refresh Dashboard first. Send a moderator your Discord ID, the message/assignment link, approximate time and what you expected. Do not repeatedly ask for a second manual award while a reaction is still queued.

If Reviews is empty, there may be no available work or you may lack the configured reviewer role. If the guide says X handle is missing, register it. If a panel expires, reopen the panel section or slash command.

Activity reports are moderator tools. They show evidence the bot has observed, including available messages and recent tracked actions. They cannot prove whether you read a channel, joined voice or wrote a message deleted before observation.
