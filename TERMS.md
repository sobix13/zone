# Melee Zone Terms of Use

Last updated: October 15, 2026

## Service
Melee Zone is a Discord application for community submissions, reviews, events, moderation, and Melee Credits (MC). Use of the bot is subject to the rules of the Discord community and applicable Discord terms.

## Participation
Participation is voluntary. Members should submit relevant, original contributions and follow task instructions. Authorized reviewers and moderators evaluate submissions. Submission does not guarantee approval or MC.

## Melee Credits
MC is an internal community participation credit, not a cryptocurrency or financial claim. MC does not promise any token, airdrop, payment, or future reward. Administrators may correct errors and duplicate awards.

## Fair use
Spam, duplicate submissions for extra credits, manipulation of reviews, impersonation, and abuse of the application are prohibited. Moderators may reject submissions or restrict access under community rules.

## Availability and changes
Features, review criteria, and tasks may change. Service availability is not guaranteed. Material changes should be communicated through official community channels.

## Privacy and contact
Read the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md). For questions, contact Melee community administrators privately through the Discord server where Melee Zone operates.
