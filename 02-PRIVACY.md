# Melee Zone Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

## Scope
Melee Zone is a Discord application for community submissions, reviews, events, moderation, and Melee Credits (MC). This policy describes data processed by the application.

## Data processed
Depending on server settings and features used, Melee Zone processes Discord user and server IDs, usernames, display names, roles, submissions, links, feedback, review outcomes, MC balances, reactions, timestamps, and audit records. It also processes message content for submissions, configured activity tracking, and administrator-requested reports, including message excerpts from accessible channel history. Optional social handles may be stored when supplied by members.

## Purpose
Data supports community submissions, reviews, participation tracking, MC accounting, role management, prevention of duplicate awards, authorized reporting, moderation, and service reliability.

## Permissions
Server Members Intent supports membership and role-based features and complete authorized member reports. Message Content Intent supports message-based submissions, activity tracking, assignment handling, and content reports.

## Storage and access
The bot stores records in a privately managed SQLite database and generates reports such as CSV and XLSX files. Authorized administrators and designated reviewers access data according to configured permissions. Discord separately processes information under its own policies.

## Retention and deletion
Records and reports may be retained for community operations, audits, dispute handling, and recovery. No uniform automatic retention period currently applies to all records. Copies may remain in backups and audit logs. To request access, correction, or deletion, contact Melee community administrators privately through the Discord server where the bot operates. Include your Discord user ID. Requests will be assessed against operational and security needs.

## Sharing and security
Data is processed through Discord and the infrastructure hosting Melee Zone. Authorized administrators and reviewers receive access needed for their responsibilities. The application is not designed to sell member data. Role-based controls, restricted storage, and audit logging support security.

## Updates and contact
This policy may change as the service evolves. For privacy questions, contact Melee community administrators privately through the Discord server where Melee Zone operates.
